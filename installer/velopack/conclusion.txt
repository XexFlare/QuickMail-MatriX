MailBoXX has been installed.

You can start MailBoXX from the Start Menu. The first time it runs, MailBoXX offers to add a desktop shortcut; you can change that choice anytime in Settings.

On first launch, open Settings, then Accounts, to add your email account. Press F1 inside the app to open the user guide.

MailBoXX will keep itself up to date automatically from now on.
